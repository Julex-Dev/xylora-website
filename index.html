Never Miss a Lead

Every missed call, answered in seconds.

AI-powered missed call recovery that texts your customers back automatically, answers their questions, captures the lead, and tells you when you need to step in.

See how it will work ↓
The Problem

A missed call is usually a lost job

You can't answer the phone while you're on a roof, under a sink, or with a client. But the customer calling you doesn't wait — they call the next business on the list.

✕
Calls go to voicemail

Most people won't leave one. They just hang up and try someone else.

✕
After-hours enquiries go unanswered

Evenings and weekends are when customers actually have time to call.

✕
Leads go cold before you follow up

By the time you get back to the ute at 5pm, they've already booked someone.

✕
You have no record of who called

No list, no follow-up, no way to know how much work you're losing.

The Solution

An AI assistant that picks up where your phone left off

We set up a system that reacts the instant a call is missed — and keeps working until the enquiry is captured or you take over.

1
The call is missed
Your phone rings out, goes to voicemail, or you're already on another job. The system notices immediately.
2
They get a text back
Within seconds the caller receives a friendly SMS from your business — apologising for the miss and asking how you can help.
3
AI answers and qualifies
Trained on your services, pricing approach and service area, it answers common questions and works out what the customer actually needs.
4
You get notified
When the enquiry is worth your time — or needs a human — you get an alert with the full conversation and their details already captured.
Benefits

What changes for your business

⏱️

Respond in seconds, not hours

The business that replies first usually wins the job. Yours now replies faster than you ever could manually.

🌙

Covered after hours

Nights, weekends and public holidays are handled the same way as a Tuesday morning.

📋

Every enquiry captured

Name, number, and what they wanted — recorded in one place instead of lost in a call log.

🎯

Fewer time-wasters

Enquiries are qualified before they reach you, so you spend your time on the jobs worth quoting.

🔔

You stay in control

You're notified when action is required. The AI handles the routine; you handle the decisions.

🧠

Trained on your business

Your services, your service area, your tone of voice — not a generic chatbot script.

Ideal For

Businesses that live and die by the phone

If most of your work starts with a phone call and you can't always answer it, this is the highest-impact thing we can build for you.

✓Trades — plumbers, electricians, builders, landscapers
✓Mobile and on-site services who can't stop to answer
✓Clinics, salons and studios with a busy front desk
✓Solo operators with no receptionist
✓Any business getting enquiries outside business hours
✓Owners who suspect they're losing work but can't prove it
FAQ

Never Miss a Lead — questions

Do I need to change my phone number?
+
No. The system works alongside your existing number, so your business cards, signage and Google listing all stay exactly as they are.
Will customers know they're talking to AI?
+
We're upfront about it — the messages come from your business and are written in your tone, and we never pretend a bot is a person. In practice customers care far more that someone replied quickly than about who typed it.
What happens if the AI can't answer something?
+
It hands over. The system is set up with clear limits — anything outside them triggers a notification to you with the full conversation, so you can jump in and reply yourself.
Can it quote prices?
+
That's your call. Some businesses want indicative pricing given out; others want every quote to come from them personally. We configure it either way during setup.
How long does setup take?
+
Most setups are live within a couple of weeks. The bulk of that is training the assistant on your services and testing it properly before it ever talks to a real customer.
What does it cost?
+
There's a one-off setup fee plus a monthly plan that covers messaging, hosting and ongoing tuning. The exact figure depends on your call volume and how much you want the assistant to handle, so we confirm it after the Discovery Call — always fixed and agreed upfront.
Can I turn it off or take over a conversation?
+
Yes. You can step into any conversation at any time, and the whole system can be paused if you're on leave or fully booked.
Do I need a website for this to work?
+
No — it works from your phone number alone. It does work better alongside a good website, because more of the enquiries it captures will already be warm, but a website isn't a requirement.

SEO

Get found by the customers already looking for you.

Improve your visibility on Google, attract more local customers, and build long-term organic growth with search engine optimisation tailored to your business.

What's involved ↓
The Problem

Being good at what you do isn't enough if nobody can find you

When someone searches for your service in your area, you're either on that first screen or you may as well not exist. Most small business websites simply aren't built to show up.

✕
Competitors outrank you

Often businesses no better than yours — they've just done the groundwork online.

✕
Your Google Business Profile is thin

Incomplete or unmanaged listings lose out on map and local pack visibility.

✕
Technical issues hold the site back

Slow pages, broken structure and missing basics quietly cap what you can rank for.

✕
You're paying for every click

Ads stop the moment you stop paying. Organic visibility keeps working.

The Solution

SEO built around your business, not a template

Every business competes in a different market, in a different area, against different competitors. We build the strategy around yours — there is no fixed package that gets applied to everyone.

How we work

We start by understanding your market and where you currently stand, then agree on what to focus on first. SEO is a long-term investment: progress is gradual, and we'd rather set honest expectations than promise you a position on Google that nobody can guarantee.

📍

Local SEO

Helping you appear for searches in the areas you actually service, so the people nearby find you first.

🔧

Technical SEO

Making sure your website is fast, crawlable and structured in a way search engines can properly understand.

🤖

AI Search Optimisation

More people now start with an AI assistant instead of a search box. We work on how your business is represented there too.

🗺️

Google Business Profile

Setting up and maintaining the listing that drives map results, reviews and calls straight from search.

🔁

Ongoing optimisation

Search doesn't stand still. We keep reviewing, refining and improving rather than treating SEO as a one-off job.

Benefits

Why it's worth the patience

🌱

Compounding, not rented

Unlike ads, the work you do this quarter keeps paying off next year.

🎯

Intent-driven enquiries

Someone searching for your service in your suburb is already close to buying.

📞

More calls from maps

A well-managed Google Business Profile often drives more direct calls than the website itself.

⚡

A faster, healthier website

Technical SEO work improves the experience for real visitors, not just crawlers.

📊

Clear reporting

You'll know what was done, what moved, and what we're focusing on next — in plain English.

🛡️

No risky shortcuts

No bought links, no keyword stuffing, nothing that puts your site at risk down the track.

Ideal For

Businesses playing the long game

SEO rewards consistency. It suits businesses that want a steady flow of enquiries built up over time, rather than a spike this week.

✓Local service businesses competing in a defined area
✓Businesses relying too heavily on paid ads
✓Anyone with a website that gets almost no traffic
✓Clinics, trades and studios that live on local search
✓Businesses expanding into new suburbs or regions
✓Owners who want honest reporting, not vanity metrics
FAQ

SEO — questions

Can you guarantee I'll rank number one on Google?
+
No, and you should be cautious of anyone who does. Nobody controls Google's rankings. What we can do is the work that genuinely improves your visibility, and report honestly on how it's tracking.
How long before I see results?
+
SEO is gradual. Some technical and Google Business Profile improvements can show up relatively quickly, while competitive search terms take months of consistent work. We'll be straight with you about what's realistic in your market.
Is SEO the same for every business?
+
Not at all. Strategies are tailored to each business — your industry, your service area, your competition and your current starting point all change what we prioritise.
What does SEO cost?
+
It depends entirely on your market and how competitive it is, so we don't publish set prices. We'll scope it properly after the Discovery Call and give you a clear figure before any work starts.
What is AI Search Optimisation?
+
A growing share of people now ask an AI assistant for recommendations instead of scrolling search results. That means making sure your business information is accurate, consistent and clearly structured wherever those tools look for it.
Do I need a new website to do SEO?
+
Usually not. We'll assess what you have first. If the existing site has fundamental problems that would cap results, we'll tell you honestly rather than charging you for SEO on a foundation that can't support it.
Do I have to sign a long contract?
+
No lock-in contracts. SEO does need consistency to work, so we'll be upfront that stopping after a month won't achieve much — but you're never trapped.
What will you actually report on?
+
The things that matter to your business: visibility for the searches you care about, traffic, and the enquiries and calls coming through. Not a wall of numbers designed to look impressive.

FAQ

Websites — questions

How long does a website take?
+

What does it cost?
+
