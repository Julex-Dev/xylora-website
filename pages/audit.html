What we cover

A thorough look at your online presence.

Every Discovery Call covers these four areas so you leave with a clear picture of what’s working, what isn’t, and what to do next.

1

Website crawl & speed test

We run your site through professional SEO and performance tools to identify technical issues, broken links, slow load times, and mobile problems.

2

Conversion review

We manually review your website from a customer’s perspective, evaluating clarity, trust signals, calls to action, and the likelihood of a visitor becoming a lead.

3

SEO & visibility check

We check your search rankings, keyword opportunities, and Google Business Profile against your top 3 local competitors to show where you’re winning and losing.

4

Report & recommendations

After the call you receive a clear written report with prioritised recommendations. We flag quick wins, important fixes, and longer-term opportunities, with honest impact estimates for each.
