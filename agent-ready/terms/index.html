Agent-Ready service terms

Last updated 25 November 2026. These terms are between Xylora Digital (ABN 42 170 939 238, a sole trader business in Springwood NSW), "we", and the business buying the service, "you".

1. What we provide

Agent-Ready has two parts. Setup is a one-off service: we check and correct your public business profiles, add structured business information and a prices page to your website, set up a quote-by-photo and booking path, and give you a before-and-after report. Care is a monthly service: each month we re-check how AI assistants describe your business, keep your prices and hours up to date on the pages we manage, send you a one-page report and make one improvement. Anything not listed here is not included.

2. The free AI Check

The AI Check asks AI assistants set questions about your business and reports what they said. Assistants give different answers on different days, so the check is a sample, not a measurement of every answer.

3. Prices and payment

Setup is paid up front. Care is billed monthly by card through Stripe. Founding prices ($99 setup, $79 a month) are locked for 12 months from your payment date for the first 10 customers. After that, or for later customers, our then-current prices apply, and we'll tell you at least 30 days before any change. We're not registered for GST, so no GST is added to these prices.

4. No guarantee of results

We don't promise rankings, mentions, leads or jobs. AI assistants and search tools are run by other companies, they change often, and any change we make can take weeks to show up or may not show up at all.

5. What we need from you

Accurate business details, prices and hours, and access to your website and profiles where needed (shared through the onboarding form or the platform's own access settings, never by sending us passwords in a message). We deliver within 5 business days of receiving what we need. You confirm you have the right to give us that access.

6. Using AI

We use AI tools to do and check parts of the work. A person at Xylora reviews everything before it reaches you. We don't make claims about your business that you haven't confirmed.

7. Changes to scope

If something extra comes up, we'll tell you and agree the cost with you before doing it. We won't add charges without your yes.

8. Cancelling

Care is month to month. You can cancel any time. It continues until the end of the month you've already paid for, then stops. Setup can be refunded in full only if you ask before we've started work. Once work has started, Setup isn't refundable. We don't refund part-months of Care. Nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

9. Support

Questions about your setup are answered free for 30 days after delivery. After that, extra support is quoted before we start.

10. Ownership and your information

What we put on your website and profiles belongs to you and stays when you cancel. We keep our methods and tools. We use your business information only to deliver the service.

11. Our liability

To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you've paid us in the 3 months before the problem arose, and we aren't liable for lost profit or lost business. Nothing here removes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law.

12. Changes and law
