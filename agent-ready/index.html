Questions

Is this SEO?

No. It's about AI assistants, though much of the same groundwork helps Google too. If you want SEO as well, we offer that separately.

Is Agent-Ready just GEO?

GEO is the first step: getting named in AI answers. Agent-Ready includes that and goes on to the three steps after it, so an AI can answer questions about you, quote you and send people to book. Neither can guarantee a spot in any answer.

Do you use AI to do this?

Yes. We use AI tools, and we tell you so. A person at Xylora checks everything before it reaches you.

Could I do this myself?

Parts of it, yes. Setup is about three hours of careful work most tradies don't have, so we do it for you.

What happens if I cancel?

Care stops at the end of the month you've paid for. Everything we set up on your site and profiles stays yours.

What's llms.txt?

A small optional file we add during setup because it's cheap. Early evidence says AI tools rarely request it, so we don't count on it.
