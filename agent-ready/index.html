The problem, in plain words

Ask an AI assistant for a tradie in your suburb and it names two or three businesses. If your profiles are patchy, your prices aren't published and there's no easy way to book, it names someone else.

In our own check of 40 local trade businesses around the Blue Mountains and Penrith, 7 weren't named at all across 18 test answers, and many of the rest were named by only one assistant. It's a small local sample, not a study, and AI answers change from day to day.

Who it isn't for

Businesses with a call centre, franchises, and anyone who wants a guaranteed ranking. We can't promise that and we won't.

What we don't promise

We can't promise more jobs or a spot in any AI answer. The assistants decide that, and changes take weeks to show up. What we do promise is that your information is complete, consistent and easy to find, and that every month you'll see for yourself what the assistants say about you.
